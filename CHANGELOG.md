# Changelog

## 3.0.0 — Flagship release

A ground-up rebuild of the table, the interface, the audio and most of the engine.

### Table and presentation
- New semicircular table: padded leather rail, brass bead, wood apron, dealer counter and chip rack, table-limit
  placard, a rebuilt shoe (smoked lid, visible next card) and an acrylic discard holder that fills as the shoe runs.
- The felt print is painted at runtime from the active rules (3:2 or 6:5, S17 or H17, side-bet circles), in four
  felt colours, and baked to a mipmapped texture with a fibre/sheen shader.
- Original art-deco cards (classic and four-colour decks, four card backs) on rounded 3D card meshes; original clay
  chips with textured faces and edge bands.
- A furnished card room: pendant lamp with soft shadows, lamp-lit credenza, wainscoting and wallpaper, framed art,
  lit sign, back bar and curtains. Depth of field, SSAO, SSIL and volumetric light by quality tier.
- Real dealing choreography: cards leave the shoe in draw order, the dealer peeks under an ace or ten, flips the hole
  card and draws one at a time; split hands get their second card when played; doubled cards land sideways; the
  table is swept into the discard holder.
- Chip choreography: bets drop onto the circles; winnings are paid from the rack; losses collected; pushes returned.
- Camera rig with seated, overhead and menu-orbit views, split-hand focus, sway, parallax and a blackjack push-in.

### Game
- Configurable table rules: 1/2/4/6/8 decks, S17/H17, 3:2/6:5, double after split, re-split aces, split to 2/3/4,
  late surrender, penetration 60–85 %, side bets on/off, four presets and a live house-edge estimate.
- Side bets: Perfect Pairs (25/12/6) and 21+3 (100/40/30/10/5) with exact published house edges per deck count.
- Even money when you hold a blackjack against an ace; table limits $1–$10,000 (side bets $1,000); rebuy when broke.
- Basic-strategy engine for the active rules, HINT button, optional coach that grades every decision, generated
  strategy chart and lifetime strategy accuracy.
- Hi-Lo card-counting trainer with running and true count.
- Hand history (last 60 rounds), expanded statistics with a session bankroll chart, 26 achievements.
- Split hands are dealt realistically; a mid-round shoe exhaustion reshuffles only the discards.

### Interface
- New theme with bundled Inter and Noto Serif Display fonts; main menu over an orbiting table; tabbed settings with
  live apply; table-rules editor; statistics, history, achievements and strategy-chart pages; confirm dialogs.
- New HUD: bankroll with count-up, rules strip, shoe gauge with cut-card marker, chip rack, contextual action bar
  with key hints, bet summary, floating hand badges, result banner, side-bet callouts, coach and achievement toasts.
- Six-step guided tour; gamepad support; UI scale, reduce-motion, camera-sway and four-colour-deck options.

### Audio
- Original synthesized lounge-jazz and menu music, room ambience and 29 effects, generated by
  `tools/generate_audio.py`, with variation, pitch jitter and music crossfades.

### Engineering
- O(1) shoe bookkeeping with integer card ids (200k-hand simulation in ~90 s instead of ~160 s with far more checks).
- 400+ assertions across engine, features and persistence; a 200,000-hand simulation across five rule sets that
  checks money conservation every round; a headless end-to-end smoke test of the real game scene; an optional
  1,000,000-hand basic-strategy edge check.
- Settings schema v3 and statistics schema v2 with automatic migration of 1.x/2.x saves, atomic writes and
  corrupt-file quarantine. History and achievements are stored separately.
- GitHub Actions CI; scripted QA/screenshot harness; reproducible asset generators.

## 2.0.0

- Reframed the table as a deeper green, black, and gold Shadowfetch private-table environment with branded architecture, tighter camera composition, ACES lighting, and restrained quality-tier bloom.
- Enlarged cards and moved both player and dealer layouts into the seated camera's strongest readable area while retaining a face-down dealer hole card.
- Added optional late surrender and configurable H17 play; S17 remains the default. Both rules are persisted and lock in on the next deal.
- Added surrender UI, keyboard control, settlement feedback, statistics accounting, and randomized-simulation coverage.
- Sequenced action presentation so hit/double/split/dealer cards finish arriving before result reveal and cleanup.
- Completed settings control synchronization and grouped table rules in the settings screen.
- Added a reproducible versioned Linux tarball/checksum packager and a user-local uninstall path that preserves saves.
- Expanded the full suite to 172 assertions and 200,000 complete randomized hands.

## 1.0.0

- Standalone Linux 3D blackjack with seated first-person camera
- Readable tilted card faces (player hands and dealer up-card); hole card stays down until reveal
- Six-deck shoe, S17, 3:2 blackjack, DAS, split to four hands, conventional split aces, insurance
- Fictional bankroll, persisted stats and settings
- User-local install: `~/.local/bin/shadowfetch-blackjack`
